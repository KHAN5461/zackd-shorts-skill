# Zack D Films & ReachMora 3D Shorts — AI Skill & Director Package

An enterprise-grade AI Skill for **Claude**, **Antigravity**, **Claude Code**, **Cursor**, **Windsurf**, and **Custom GPTs** designed to generate viral, ultra-high-retention 3D explainer YouTube Shorts and TikToks in the signature styles of **Zack D Films** and **ReachMora**.

---

## 🚀 Key Capabilities

1. **Zack D Explainer Engine (10 Scenes):** Continuous cause-and-effect narrative with strictly 8 to 12 words per scene.
2. **Historical Thriller & Mystery Engine (4–6 Scenes):** True, eerie, and unbelievable historical stories (strange inventions, bizarre medical practices, forgotten disasters, extreme survival).
3. **Script Rewriting & Transcript Polishing:** Cleans raw YouTube video transcripts, eliminates timestamps and filler words, and sharpens dramatic tension.
4. **AAA 3D Game Cutscene Prompts:** Realistic 3D animated game cutscene aesthetics (Unreal Engine 5.4 / Unity HDRP) with persistent character and period anchors.
5. **AI Video Animation Mastery:** Timestamped micro-choreography (`0–2s`, `2–4s`, `4–6s`) for **Grok AI**, **Google Veo 3**, **Kling**, **Wan 2.1**, **Sora**, and **Minimax Hailuo**.
6. **Free Production Toolstack Pipeline:** Step-by-step guidance utilizing ElevenLabs, Grok AI, and CapCut.
