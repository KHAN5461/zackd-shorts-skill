## 2. Virality Rating Algorithm
Virality scores (1.0 to 10.0) are calculated using 3 metrics:
1. **Initial Curiosity Tension (40%):** Does the title trigger an irresistible question in under 1.5 seconds?
2. **Visual Potential (40%):** Can this be animated as a dynamic 3D cutaway or escalating simulation?
3. **Broad Demographic Appeal (20%):** Is the topic understandable to any global viewer regardless of age?
