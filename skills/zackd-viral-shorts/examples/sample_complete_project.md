# Complete End-to-End Production Example

## Project Topic: *What Happens If You Fall Into a Grain Silo?*

---

### STAGE 1: 10-Scene Script (8–12 Words Per Scene)

- **Scene 1 (10 words):** A worker accidentally steps onto dry grain inside a massive silo.
- **Scene 2 (10 words):** People assume dry grain acts like soft, safe stepping sand.
- **Scene 3 (10 words):** However, moving corn behaves exactly like deadly industrial quicksand below.
- **Scene 4 (10 words):** Within seconds, shifting kernels pull his legs downward without friction.
- **Scene 5 (10 words):** Struggling only compacts the grain tighter around his entire lower body.
- **Scene 6 (10 words):** The immense friction creates thousands of pounds of crushing pressure.
- **Scene 7 (10 words):** Each panicked breath allows heavy corn kernels to pack tighter.
- **Scene 8 (10 words):** Pulling him straight up would actually dislocate his upper spine.
- **Scene 9 (10 words):** Rescuers must slide a metal protective cylinder around his torso.
- **Scene 10 (10 words):** Vacuuming the isolated interior grain finally releases all inward pressure.
